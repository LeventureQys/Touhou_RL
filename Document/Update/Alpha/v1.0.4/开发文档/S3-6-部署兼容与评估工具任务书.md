## 证据消费字段（不得凭文件名推断）

evidence固定9键：state_recheck、policy_full_val、cross_replay、eval_report、pytest、d9、d10、d11、startup。每项{path,sha256}指工作区相对路径JSON索引。索引schema=v104-evidence/1，必需kind/status(pass|fail|blocked)/created_at/producer/command/cwd/exit_code/artifacts([{path,sha256}])/observations/reason；索引及其原始artifact都核sha，布尔status不能代替原始数据。

observations最小字段：state_recheck=checkpoint_sha256/frames/coverage_sha256/player_pos_px/danger_corr_perframe_median/danger_corr_perframe_valid；policy_full_val含相同感知项及by_danger(含frames/top3/regret)，checkpoint必须冻结policy；cross_replay=checkpoint_sha256/a_mae_raw/b_mae_raw/a_indices_sha256/b_indices_sha256/a_frames/b_frames/sampling_mode/per_stage；eval_report=checkpoint_sha256/episodes/frames_mean/hit_step_cap/episode_fields_complete，artifacts关联实际eval-report；pytest=passed/failed/skipped/command_exit及原始全套日志；d9=expected_frames/actual_frames/visited_index_sha256/last_batch_size；d10=budget_bytes/max_resident_bytes/lru_verified/value_equal及单测日志；d11=expected_files/validated_files/bytes_total/value_equal/manifest_sha；startup=real_game_started/pixels_only/no_crash/no_exception_log/ui_checked/screenshots。缺键或身份不符blocked，实际阈值不达fail，不能把status自行填pass当验收。S3-7只冻结evidence空map，S3-8补实际索引，Stage4另目录复验不覆盖原始证据。
